Get rewarded for representing your community!

You receive points every time you take a survey. Every 1,000 points has the same value as $1. Sign up and receive at least $25 (25,000 points) right away when you complete your first survey. Then, get up to $5 or more for every survey you take after. Redeem your points for Mastercard® Reward Cards, a variety of virtual gift cards for retailers such as Amazon.com, Apple, Starbucks, and more.

Your opinions will influence government and community policymakers nationwide and make a difference to the AAPI community.
